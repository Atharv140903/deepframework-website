Why most enterprise AI stalls

Most companies have tried AI. Few have it running in daily operations. The same three things get in the way.

01

Pilots that never reach production

A demo impresses in a meeting, then stops when it meets real data, real users and real approvals.

02

Systems you cannot replace

Your ERP, hospital software and accounting tools run the business. Any AI has to work with them, not around them.

03

Data spread everywhere

The information you need sits in databases, Excel files, scanned PDFs, email and WhatsApp.

How we work

We follow the same four steps on every engagement, from understanding the process to running it day to day.

  1. Step 1

    Discover

    We learn your process, review your data and systems, and agree on one result to measure.

    First call, then about 1 week
  2. Step 2

    Prototype

    A working version on your real data, so you can see it before committing to the full build.

    1 to 3 weeks
  3. Step 3

    Build

    We build the production version, connect it to your systems and train your team.

    Usually 4 to 12 weeks
  4. Step 4

    Support

    We monitor it, fix issues and improve it as your needs change.

    Ongoing, optional
